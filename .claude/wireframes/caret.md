## Behavior

- The demo closes the opening rather than interrupting it, so a reader sees the palette open before the prose describes it. It plays while hovered, and on a pointer that cannot hover it plays while it is on screen.
- Prose holds the route measure and the demo breaks past it, which is the arrangement every project route shares. Both scale with the viewport from the widest breakpoint up.
- The recording is a zoomed-out browser view with a small palette centred in it, so the poster frame reads as mostly empty at full width. Cropping to a tighter asset is the fix and it has not shipped.
- The rail lists the three section names in lowercase, matching the headings on this surface rather than the sentence case the landing rail carries.
