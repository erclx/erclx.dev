# Cast

## Overview

Owns the dog on the looking-for rule. It is drawn as inline SVG on its own and is decoration, so it carries `aria-hidden` and no accessible name. The experience margins hold no figures, since the cell-grid cast that stood there was retired.

## Layout

### `src/components/site/looking-for/`

Holds the closing ask and the dog on its rule. The rule is a curved contour at the hero field's peak weight, summing three sines so it reads as part of the surface behind it.

- The dog is clipped by a polygon built from the curve's own path, since a rectangular clip drops its grip where the curve leaves the midline
- The window sits 2rem in from the rule's end below 360px, 4rem to 768px, and 8rem from there, so he stands on full ink rather than the fade, and below 360px a deeper inset puts his ear over the Open to work label
- The amplitude ramps from 5px to 16px across a 342px to 768px stage, since a fixed one tips the dog at 390
- The fill reads `--figure-fill`, `#d4a574` in both themes, the token the about aircraft and racket read, with `#2a1d14` for the eye

## Gotchas

- Judge the dog at the size it ships, since its strokes are drawn for that scale
